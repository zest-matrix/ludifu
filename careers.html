Questions

Before You Apply.

Are these roles remote or on-site?
All roles, internship and full-time, are on-site at our Lower Parel West office in Mumbai. Office hours are 10:30 AM to 7:30 PM. If you are not based in Mumbai, please factor in relocation before applying.
Can I apply for more than one role?
Yes. Send a separate WhatsApp message for each role you're genuinely interested in, so we can route your CV correctly.
Can an internship convert to a full-time role?
Yes. Strong performers are regularly considered for full-time positions or extensions, based on business need at the time. It is not guaranteed, but it is a genuine path — several roles listed here are open to both internship and full-time candidates from the outset.
Do you offer a certificate or letter?
Yes — a completion certificate and a letter of recommendation are provided at the end of a satisfactorily completed internship.
I am still studying — can I work around my college schedule?
Yes. If you have lectures, practicals or exams, a reduced schedule can be agreed upfront — the application form asks for your timings precisely so we can plan around them. Raise it when you apply rather than after joining.
Why do you ask about travel time?
Because it is the single most common reason people leave in the first month. A 90-minute each-way commute sounds manageable in September and stops being manageable by October. We would rather know upfront and plan around it than lose you three weeks in.
Who do I actually report to?
Directly to the Founder, or to whoever the Founder designates for your specific role. There is no additional management layer at LUDIFU's current size.
