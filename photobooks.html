What Is a Coffee Table Book?

More Than an Album.
An Object of Art.

A coffee table book is a large-format, hardbound book designed to be picked up, leafed through, and admired. Unlike a digital gallery, it is a physical object — something guests reach for, something children inherit, something that makes a room feel more alive.

01
Designed Around Your Story
No templates, no stock layouts. Every spread is composed specifically around your photographs, timeline, and the feeling you want to preserve.
02
Built to Last Generations
Premium print stock, hardcover boards, and precision binding mean your book looks as good in thirty years as it does today.
03
Exclusively Yours — One of One
Every book we make is a single edition. No two are alike. Yours will never be replicated.

The Craft

How Your Book
is Made.

This is not print-and-ship. Every book goes through a meticulous, hands-on process — which is why it looks, feels, and lasts the way it does.

01
The Conversation
We begin by understanding your story. What is this book about? Who is it for? What feeling should someone have when they open it? Done over WhatsApp — no forms, no portals. Just a human discussion about something personal.
What Happens
  • You reach us on WhatsApp (+91 98678 00451)
  • We understand your occasion and vision
  • Sizing, binding, and page count explained
  • Transparent quote before you proceed
  • Advance payment confirms your slot
02
You Send. We Curate.
Send us your photographs from your phone, camera, or Drive — don't worry about order or resolution. Our team reviews every image, selects the ones that print beautifully, and sequences them to tell your story the right way.
Image Handling
  • Phone and DSLR photos both accepted
  • Colour-correction and print optimisation
  • We identify best images for full spreads
  • Blurry photos flagged before design begins
  • Works with as few as 30 photos
03
Design & Layout
Our designer builds every spread by hand — scale, flow, white space, and how your eye travels across the page. Captions, titles, quotes placed with typographic precision. No template is ever used. No two pages look the same.
Design Includes
  • Full custom layout per spread
  • Font selection matched to your tone
  • Captions, dates, names, favourite quotes
  • Full-bleed, paired, and accent pages
  • Cover design with your choice of finish
04
Your Approval
Before a single page is printed, you see everything. A complete digital proof of every spread is shared. You review at your own pace. Nothing goes to print without your explicit yes.
Approval Process
  • Full-resolution PDF proof shared
  • Page-by-page feedback welcome
  • 2 revision cycles included
  • Written approval before production
05
Premium Print Production
Your files go to high-resolution professional printing. We use calibrated equipment for colour accuracy. The difference is visible the moment you hold the pages — the weight, the sharpness, the depth of colour.
Print Specs
  • Professional high-res print output
  • 200–250 GSM premium art paper
  • Colour-managed for true-to-life tones
  • Each page inspected before binding
06
Binding & Finishing
The pages are gathered, aligned, and bound by skilled hands. The cover boards are applied, the spine reinforced, and the whole book pressed and set. This is where it stops being pages and becomes a single, unified object — your book.
Finishing
  • Hardcover board with linen or laminate
  • Reinforced spine for lay-flat opening
  • Gold or silver foil titling available
  • Slip case available on request
07
Packaging & Delivery
Your book is wrapped in tissue, placed in protective packaging, and dispatched with care. You receive a tracking number the moment it leaves our hands. Mumbai deliveries handled personally where possible.
Delivery
  • Protective book packaging
  • Tracking number on WhatsApp at dispatch
  • Mumbai: 1–2 days post-dispatch
  • Pan-India: 4–7 working days
Craftsmanship

The Art of Binding.

The binding gives a book its soul — how it opens, how it sits, how it feels in your hands. Three styles, each suited to a different kind of book.

Lay-Flat Binding
Recommended · Coffee Table Books

Pages open completely flat with no gutter — perfect for panoramic photographs and full-spread designs. Zero image loss into the spine. The professional standard for coffee table books.

Full-spread photos with zero image loss
Book lies completely flat on any surface
Superior durability — pages won't crack
The professional standard for photobooks
Hardcover Case Binding
Classic · Formal · Corporate

Rigid hardcover boards, reinforced spine, pages sewn into signatures. Conveys permanence and formality. Cover options include linen, leatherette, or laminated paper with foil titling.

Classic, prestigious appearance
Ideal for corporate and heritage books
Cover options: linen, leatherette, foil
Highly durable — built for shelf display
Thread-Sewn Binding
Heritage · Archival · Heirloom

Signatures sewn together with thread — the oldest, most durable method. Thread-sewn books open thousands of times without weakening. For books meant to outlive you.

Strongest, most archival binding method
Opens nearly flat despite traditional style
Used in rare book production worldwide
Ideal for legacy and heirloom books
