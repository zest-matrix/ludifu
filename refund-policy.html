Refund & Cancellation Policy

We want every experience with LUDIFU to be a good one. This policy covers cancellations, refunds, and credits across all our products and services — clearly and fairly.

Last Updated: March 2026
Jurisdiction: Mumbai, India

1. LUDIFU Academy — Paid Courses

🎓 LUDIFU Academy Courses Refund Eligible

Cancellation by the Learner

We understand that circumstances change. If you need to cancel your enrolment, the following terms apply:

  • Within 48 hours of enrolment — Full refund, provided no live session has been attended and course materials have not been accessed.
  • After 48 hours but before the first session — A credit note of equivalent value will be issued, valid for 6 months, redeemable against any LUDIFU Academy course.
  • After the first session has commenced — No refund or credit will be issued. The seat is considered utilised.
🕐
Refund Processing Time: 5–7 Working Days
Credited directly to the original payment source (bank account / UPI / card)

Session Rescheduling

If LUDIFU reschedules or cancels a session, we will offer either a make-up session or, if the course is discontinued entirely, a full refund processed within 5–7 working days.

Non-Attendance

Non-attendance at scheduled sessions does not entitle the learner to a refund. Recordings are made available where applicable as part of the enrolled course package.

How to Request a Refund

Contact us on WhatsApp at +91 98678 00451 or write to us via the contact form on ludifu.com within the eligible window. Please include your full name, enrolled course name, date of payment, and reason for cancellation.

Refunds will be processed to the original payment method only. Third-party payment timelines (bank processing) may vary slightly but we initiate all refunds within 2 working days of approval.

2. LUDIFU Academy — Free Workshops

Note: LUCADEMY is currently closed to open public batches. The terms below apply to corporate and group training arranged on request, and to any workshops offered in future.

✅ Free Workshops No Payment · N/A

Free workshops involve no payment and therefore no refund process applies. Seats are limited — if you are unable to attend, we request that you cancel your registration at least 12 hours in advance via WhatsApp so the seat can be offered to another learner.

LUDIFU reserves the right to cancel or reschedule free workshops at any time without liability.

3. LUDIFU PhotoBooks

📖 Custom PhotoBooks & Coffee Table Books Conditional Refund

PhotoBooks are custom-made, bespoke products designed and produced exclusively for each customer. Due to the custom nature of these orders, our refund policy is more limited than standard retail.

Before Design Approval (Pre-Production)

If you decide to cancel your order before providing final design approval, you are eligible for a refund as follows:

  • Advance / booking payment — Refundable in full if no design work has commenced.
  • Advance paid after design work has started — A design service charge may be deducted. The remaining balance will be refunded.
🕐
Refund Processing Time: 7–10 Working Days
Credited to the original payment source (bank account / UPI)

After Design Approval (Post-Production)

Once you have provided written or WhatsApp approval of the final design and production has commenced, no refund is possible. This includes:

  • Orders in print production.
  • Completed and dispatched orders.
  • Orders where the customer has changed their mind post-approval.

Please review all design proofs carefully before giving approval. LUDIFU is not responsible for errors in customer-supplied content (photos, text, names, dates) that were approved by the customer.

Defective or Damaged Orders

If you receive a PhotoBook that is defective due to a production error on our part, please contact us within 5 days of receipt with photographs of the damage. We will arrange a replacement at no additional charge. Replacements are not eligible for cash refunds.

How to Request a Cancellation

Contact us on WhatsApp at +91 98678 00451 before your design approval. Include your order reference and reason for cancellation.

4. General Refund Terms

  • All refunds are processed in Indian Rupees (INR) to the original payment source only.
  • LUDIFU does not offer cash refunds. All refunds are processed via the original digital payment method.
  • Processing timelines begin from the date of refund approval by LUDIFU, not from the date of request.
  • Bank processing times may vary. If you do not receive your refund within the stated window, please contact your bank before reaching out to us.
  • LUDIFU reserves the right to decline a refund request that does not meet the eligibility criteria stated in this policy.

For Clicarity and WA.Expert refund policies, please refer to the respective websites: clicarity.com and wa.expert.
