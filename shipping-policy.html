Shipping Policy

This policy covers how LUDIFU delivers its products and services — both physical (PhotoBooks) and digital (Academy courses, workshops, and other services).

Effective Date: 1st January 2025
Last Updated: March 2026
Jurisdiction: Mumbai, India

Overview — How LUDIFU Delivers

LUDIFU operates across digital services and physical products. The delivery method depends on the product or service you have purchased.

💻
Digital Delivery
LUDIFU Academy courses, workshops, recordings, and all other software-based services are delivered digitally via Zoom links, email, and WhatsApp. No physical shipping applies.
📦
Physical Shipping
LUDIFU PhotoBooks are physical, custom-made products and are shipped via courier to your provided delivery address across India.

1. Digital Services — LUDIFU Academy

Course Access

Upon successful enrolment and payment confirmation, you will receive your Zoom session link and onboarding details via WhatsApp and/or email within 1 working day.

Workshop Access

Where a workshop or training session is offered, you will receive your session link via WhatsApp within 24 hours of registration, and a reminder will be sent 1 hour before the session begins.

Recordings & Course Materials

Where included as part of your course, recordings and supplementary materials are shared via a secure link within 48 hours of each live session.

There are no shipping charges for any digital LUDIFU Academy product. All course access is delivered digitally.

Packaging

Every PhotoBook is packed in protective custom packaging designed to ensure it arrives in perfect condition. We take full responsibility for damage caused during transit due to inadequate packaging on our part.

Delivery Address

Please ensure your delivery address is accurate and complete at the time of order. LUDIFU is not responsible for failed deliveries due to an incorrect or incomplete address provided by the customer. Re-delivery charges, if any, will be borne by the customer.

Tracking

Once your order is dispatched, a tracking number and courier name will be shared with you via WhatsApp. You can track your order directly on the courier's website.

During festive seasons (Diwali, Christmas, New Year) and peak periods, production and delivery timelines may be extended by 3–5 additional working days. We will notify you of any delays proactively.

Damaged or Lost in Transit

In the unlikely event your PhotoBook arrives damaged or is lost in transit, please contact us within 5 days of the expected delivery date with your order reference and photographs (if damaged). We will investigate with the courier and arrange a replacement where applicable.
